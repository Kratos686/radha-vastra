🌼Our Story

Krishna Vastra began with a simple passion for creating beautiful handmade Krishna dresses, crochet creations, and stitched products. Every item is crafted with patience, care, and devotion, ensuring quality that reflects both tradition and craftsmanship


What started as a small home-based effort is growing into a mission to bring handmade artistry to every home while supporting women with meaningful work opportunities.
